    How to play

    The heist

    • Light up every crystal with the laser. The beam passes through crystals, so one beam can light several.
    • Drag on the board to rotate the mirror, laser or glass that's picked. Use ↺ ↻ for small turns.
    • Things you can turn have a dashed ring. Walls stop the laser.
    • When every crystal is lit, the vault asks for a code: a question about the angles your beam just made. Each wrong code costs 1 ★.
    • Early levels show i and r. Later levels only show the angle between the ray and the mirror, so you have to work out i and r yourself.
    • Calculate the shot levels lock the mirror: type the angle of incidence needed, then fire.

    Colours

    Change the screen theme with the colour dots at the top, and the laser colour under the board once you unlock more.

    Stars

    • Light the crystals within the par time for 3 ★, within double the par time for 2 ★, or any time for 1 ★.
    • Each wrong vault code costs 1 ★ (you always keep at least 1).
    • Each hint costs 1 ★ from that level.
    • Collect stars to unlock new laser colours.

    Quick Fire

    10 questions with no time limit on reflection, refraction and using mirrors to see round walls. Faster answers earn a bigger speed bonus, and answers right in a row build a streak bonus. You never lose points. At the end, your score gives you a rank, from Light Learner up to Speed of Light.

    The physics

    Reflection: angle of incidence = angle of reflection. Both are measured from the normal.
    Into a denser medium (air → glass or water): light slows down and bends towards the normal.
    Into a less dense medium (glass → air): light speeds up and bends away from the normal.
    Along the normal: light goes straight through without bending.
