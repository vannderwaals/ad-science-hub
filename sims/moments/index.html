        How to play

        Playing a level

        • Tap a weight in the tray, then tap a spot on the beam to place it.
        • Tap a spot with no weight picked to take its top weight off.
        • On "find the weight" and "find the distance" levels, type your answer and press Check.
        • Balance the beam: anticlockwise moments = clockwise moments.

        Timer

        Every level and exam question has 60 seconds. The faster you finish, the bigger your speed bonus. If time runs out, you can still finish, but you get no gold and no chest.
